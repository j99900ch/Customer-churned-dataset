📊 Customer Churn Prediction & Retention Analysis

Customer Churn Prediction is a practical Data Science and Machine Learning project built to understand why customers leave a business and identify customers who may be at risk of leaving in the future.

The project starts with a raw customer dataset and converts it into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, analysis, visualization, feature engineering, and machine learning. The data is carefully explored to find patterns related to customer tenure, contracts, monthly charges, payment methods, services, demographics, and overall customer behavior.

🔍 What Does the Project Analyze?

The project uses multiple charts and visualizations to answer important business questions:

👥 Which type of customers are more likely to leave?
💰 Does higher monthly spending affect churn?
📅 Does customer tenure influence retention?
📝 Which contract types have higher churn?
💳 Does payment method affect customer loyalty?
📦 Which services are connected with better retention?
🎯 Which customers should the business focus on first?

🤖 Machine Learning

The project applies classification-based machine learning to predict whether a customer is likely to stay or churn. Model performance is evaluated using metrics such as Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and Confusion Matrix.

Rather than focusing only on prediction accuracy, the project also tries to understand which customer characteristics are contributing to churn, making the results more useful from a business perspective.

💡 Business Value

The final goal is not just to say “this customer may churn.”

It is to help answer:

“Why might this customer leave, and what can the business do about it?”

By identifying high-risk customer groups and their behavioral patterns, businesses can develop targeted retention strategies such as personalized offers, better customer support, loyalty programs, and proactive engagement.
